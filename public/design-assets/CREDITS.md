## Campus welcome photos

The welcome sequence pairs each greeting with a photograph of that location. The three additional photographs below were downloaded as 1280px Wikimedia Commons thumbnails on October 13, 2026. The preview crops their display and applies grayscale in CSS; no people or event details have been generated or substituted.

- `campus.jpg` — Japan House garden, Gayatri Pandkar. [Original photo](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-park-bench-sitting-next-to-a-lake-FgGkFDpLfPQ), [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). The photographer's page identifies Champaign and tags Japan House/UIUC. Published October 31, 2023.
- `state-farm-center.jpg` — State Farm Center exterior, Amphylite, September 9, 2007. [Original and attribution](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:StateFarmCenter.jpg), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). - `busey-evans.jpg` — Busey Hall, part of Busey-Evans Residence Halls, Dori, March 23, 2008. [Original and attribution](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:BuseyHall_Urbana_Illinois_4543.jpg), [CC BY-SA 3.0 US](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/us/). The cropped, grayscale display is available under the same license.
- `ikenberry.jpg` — Ikenberry library, University of Illinois Library; photograph by C. E. Crane, December 18, 2013 (the file metadata records December 17). [Original and attribution](<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ike_Library_Stacks_(11440740143).jpg>), [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/). Resized to 1280px; cropped and displayed in grayscale. The photo shows the library interior at Ikenberry, not a generic campus library.

Research note: [State Farm Center's event page](https://www.statefarmcenter.com/events/detail/summit) confirms John Summit's October 1, 2026 show. A reusable photograph from that specific show was not verified, so the welcome sequence uses the credited venue exterior. An early Ikenberry construction photo was inspected and excluded in favor of the completed library interior.
